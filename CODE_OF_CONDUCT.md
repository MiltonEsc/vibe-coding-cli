# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Vibe CLI welcomes contributors and users from different backgrounds and experience levels. Participation should be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project and helping people use it safely.

Examples of positive participation include:

- Giving specific, actionable feedback.
- Disagreeing with ideas without attacking people.
- Assuming good intent while still reviewing technical and security claims critically.
- Respecting privacy and avoiding the disclosure of credentials, personal data, or security-sensitive information.
- Helping newcomers understand the project conventions and contribution process.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory or demeaning comments, deliberate disruption, publishing private information without permission, and sustained hostile behavior toward other participants.

## Project spaces

This Code of Conduct applies to Vibe CLI repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or project.

For security vulnerabilities, do not open a public issue. Follow the private reporting instructions in `SECURITY.md`.

For other conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainer through GitHub using a private channel when available. Include enough context to understand the situation, but do not share unrelated personal information.

Maintainers should handle reports as privately and consistently as reasonably possible.
